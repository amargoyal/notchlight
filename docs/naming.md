# Naming the multipurpose companion

Reviewed September 9, 2026. **Selected: Notchlight.** The owner approved the name; the app, repository, and
checkout have been renamed together. The comparisons below record the decision. Web checks identify existing products; they do not
establish name, domain, or trademark availability.

The umbrella name should cover Claude status, music, files, and a possible
clipboard face. Keep Claude as a view name and retain its buddy. Favor a short,
easy-to-say name that suggests a useful place at the edge of the screen.

## Claude's shortlist

| Name | Product fit | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Notchlight** | Clear location, warm tone, direct continuity with Claude Light. Supports all the current faces. | Strongest candidate from this list. Slightly descriptive and ties the identity to the notch. |
| **Sill** | A familiar little shelf: a good metaphor for things kept within reach. Quiet and broad. | Best metaphor, but needs differentiation from the existing [Sill news product](https://sill.social/). |
| **Ledge** | Excellent physical shelf metaphor and easy to remember. | Avoid: [Ledge](https://github.com/CasterlyGit/ledge) already describes a Mac notch screenshot/file shelf. |
| **Lantern** | Warm and welcoming, with continuity through the light metaphor. Less connected to files or the screen edge. | Deprioritize: existing software includes [Lantern for Mac](https://lantern.io/download?os=mac) and a [Lantern desktop recording app](https://www.uselantern.io/download). |
| **Perch** | Excellent sense of a companion sitting at the top of the screen. | Avoid: [Dynamic Notch Island — Perch](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/dynamic-notch-island-perch/id6742724228?mt=12) already occupies the category; another [Perch project](https://github.com/tukuyomil032/Perch) also combines a notch hub, music, and AI usage. |

## Two additional directions

- **Brim** — the edge of the screen, a small place holding useful things.
  Shorter and more independent of the current name. My favorite new creative
  direction; generic-word software collisions still need a dedicated check.
  Possible line: “A little more within reach.”
- **Rimlet** — a small rim, with a friendly diminutive that suits the buddy.
  More distinctive in sound, though less immediately understandable than Brim
  or Notchlight. Possible line: “Your Mac's little companion.”

Quick searches did not surface an obvious notch-app match for Notchlight or
Rimlet. That is limited evidence, not confirmation they are available.

Other appealing words were screened out: [Eave already names a notch app](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/eave-life-in-your-notch/id6792812973?mt=12),
and [Tuck already names a Mac menu-bar utility](https://usetuck.com/).

## Decision

**Notchlight was selected** for recognition, continuity, and a name that covers
all three faces. Brim was the alternative considered. A concise description: “Notchlight keeps Claude, music, and files
within reach.”

The coordinated migration is documented in [the rename notes](rename.md).
A public release still needs its own domain and brand availability checks.
Do not replace Claude session/permission terminology just because the parent
app's name changes.
